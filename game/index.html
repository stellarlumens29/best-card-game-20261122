EMBER SIGIL DUEL

Four elements. Thirty cards. One Keeper to beat.

Goal: reduce the Keeper's life from 20 to 0 before yours hits 0.
Each round your mana refills (1, 2, 3 … up to 6) and you draw a card.
Cards cost mana and show their cost, power and effect — Strike deals damage, Heal restores life, Ward absorbs damage, Draw damages and draws.
Sigil: one element is strong each round (+2 damage, +1 heal/ward) and the sigil walks the wheel Ember → Thorn → Storm → Frost.
Controls: click a card (click again to play it), or keys 1–7 + Enter. E ends your turn, R restarts.

Keyboard + mouse · runs offline from static files · no network
